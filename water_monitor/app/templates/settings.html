{# ── Re-run Setup ──────────────────────────────────────────────── #}
⚠
Destructive operation Re-running the wizard lets you re-search for a device and re-confirm entity mappings. Existing event history is kept, but circuit configuration will be overwritten when you complete the wizard.

Re-run Setup Wizard

Setup mutations are normally locked after initial setup so a stray POST cannot wipe your configuration. Use this to deliberately unlock the wizard — for example after swapping hardware or moving to a new Home Assistant instance. We strongly recommend downloading a backup first.

When you confirm:

  • The setup wizard re-opens at step 0 (New / Restore).
  • You can re-discover a device and re-confirm entity mappings.
  • Setup re-locks itself automatically when you finish the wizard.
  • Event history, training data, and clusters are preserved unless you choose Restore from backup.
