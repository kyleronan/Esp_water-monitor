{# ── History & Events ──────────────────────────────────────────── #} {# These control what the History LIST shows and the background event hygiene — they used to hide inside the Display Units form, where nobody thought to look for them. Own form + endpoint so saving them can't touch the unit selection. #}

History & Events

How recorded water events are shown and tidied. These never change your water totals — only what the History list displays and how merged recordings are cleaned up.

Hide not-real-use events from History The monitor automatically flags readings that aren't real water use — city water-pressure blips (👻), draws on your other water line (↔), and tiny drips or sensor noise (Drip). Their volume is always removed from your totals; this toggle only controls whether the rows also disappear from the list (on by default). History always says how many are hidden, and relabeling a wrongly-flagged event restores its volume.
Automatically split merged events Sometimes several quick uses close together get recorded as one long event. When on (the default), the monitor rebuilds those into the separate uses once an hour (the same thing the “Reprocess” button does). Only unlabelled, clearly-merged events are touched; your labels and water totals are always preserved.
